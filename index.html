What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computing focused on creating systems that perform tasks commonly associated with human intelligence.

💡
Think of it this way

A navigation app uses data about roads and traffic to recommend a route to your destination.

Key points

  • AI systems are designed for specific tasks or goals.
  • They may use rules, search, data, or learned patterns.
  • AI outputs should be checked for errors and limitations.
